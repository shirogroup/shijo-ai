# Google Ads keyword research — SHIJO.AI (2026-08-23)

Source: Google Keyword Planner, account **643-120-9303**, "Discover new keywords",
10 seed terms drawn from the 12 live tools. United States · English · Google ·
Aug 2025 – Jul 2026. Full export: **2,445 keyword ideas**, downloaded and analysed
row by row (not sampled). Raw shortlist: `2026-08-23-keyword-clusters.csv`.

> Volumes come back as buckets (`500`, `5,000`) rather than exact figures because
> the account has almost no spend. Google's own note on screen: *"To get more
> detailed statistics, run a campaign."* Treat volumes as order-of-magnitude.

---

## 1. The blocker that outranks every keyword decision

**There are no public tool pages.** Confirmed against the route tree: the only
indexable marketing routes are `/`, `/ai-marketing-tools`, `/lp`, `/blog`,
`/blog/[slug]`, `/contact` and the legal pages. All 12 tools live at
`/dashboard/tools/[toolId]` — **behind authentication**.

Consequences, both of them measurable:

1. **Paid search has nowhere relevant to land.** Bidding on
   *"meta description generator"* and landing on a generic platform page is a
   landing-page-experience mismatch. Google prices that directly: worse Quality
   Score → higher actual CPC than the bid estimates below → fewer clicks per dollar.
2. **It explains the zero organic footprint.** Ahrefs shows **0 organic keywords**
   and 12 indexed pages. Twelve tools, zero indexable tool pages. The
   meta-description cluster alone is ~4,250 searches/month that the site cannot
   currently rank for or advertise into properly.

**One public landing page per tool is the highest-leverage change available**, and
it serves paid and organic at the same time. Everything below assumes it, or
accepts a known handicap without it.

## 5. Dynamic keyword features — not applicable

- **Dynamic Keyword Insertion** works only in Search campaigns with keywords
  (Google Ads Help, "Set up keyword insertion for your ad text"). The only live
  campaign is Performance Max, which has no keywords. Not an option today, and a
  polish tactic regardless — irrelevant at 713 impressions/month.
- **Dynamic Search Ads** are being retired into AI Max (new DSA creation ends
  ~Sept 2026, sunset Feb 2027). Do not build on DSA now.

## 6. Recommended sequence

1. **Build public tool landing pages** — one per tool, indexable, each targeting
   its own cluster. Serves paid and organic together. Prerequisite for 2 and 3.
2. **Ship the purchase event** (`{CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}` → server-side verify →
   `dataLayer.push`). Without it no keyword decision can be judged on revenue.
3. **Then** a small Search campaign: one ad group on Cluster A (phrase + exact),
   the 88 negatives applied, landing on the meta-generator page.
4. Cluster B only after the free-tool → paid path is measurable.
5. Leave head terms alone until there is conversion data to justify the CPC.
